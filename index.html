FAQ

Good to know before your first visit.

Can beginners play on the course?

Yes. Start with 9 holes or the practice range, or book the Start golf course — clubs are included.

Do I need my own clubs?

No. Rental sets are €25 and include a bag, balls and tees.

Is there a dress code?

Smart-casual golf wear: collared shirt, no denim, soft-spike shoes. Rental shoes are available.

How do I book a tee time?

Online in under a minute, or call the reception. Weekends book up 3–5 days ahead.

Can I come just for the restaurant or spa?

The Terrace is open to everyone. Spa day passes are €35.
